Bayesian endemic-epidemic model with mobility: replication of Slater et al. (2025) for Catalunya

A daily replication of model 1 of Slater et al. (2025), an endemic-epidemic model with an exogenous, an autoregressive, a neighbourhood and a mobility component, fitted in Stan to the 374 basic health areas (ABS) of Catalunya from 2020-03-22 to 2021-05-01.
